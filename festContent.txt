Welcome to TECHSPARK 2025 — the annual inter-college tech fest hosted by SPMVV!

📅 Dates: August 22–24, 2025  
📍 Venue: SPMVV Campus, Tirupati

🎯 About the Fest:
TECHSPARK is a celebration of innovation, creativity, and collaboration. Students from across India participate in coding challenges, tech talks, workshops, and cultural events.

🧠 Flagship Events:
1. CodeStorm — 24-hour hackathon with cash prizes up to ₹50,000.
2. RoboRush — Robotics competition featuring obstacle navigation and battle rounds.
3. Brainwave — Technical quiz with team rounds and buzzer battles.
4. DesignSprint — UI/UX design challenge judged by industry experts.

🎤 Guest Speakers:
- Dr. Kavya Ramesh (AI Researcher, Google DeepMind)
- Mr. Arjun Mehta (CTO, InnovateX)
- Ms. Sneha Rao (Product Designer, Microsoft)
